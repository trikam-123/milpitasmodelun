What is Model United Nations?

Model United Nations is an educational simulation in which students act as delegates representing countries or figures within United Nations committees. Students debate global issues, draft resolutions, and collaborate to find solutions while following real UN procedures.

Through this experience, participants develop skills in diplomacy, public speaking, research, and teamwork while gaining a deeper understanding of international relations and global issues.
