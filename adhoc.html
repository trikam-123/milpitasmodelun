Adhoc Committee

Ad Hoc is a fast-paced, secret-topic crisis committee designed to challenge delegates' creativity, adaptability, and critical thinking. Unlike traditional Model UN committees, delegates do not know the committee topic before the conference. Instead, they receive the scenario at the start of committee and must quickly analyze the situation, develop strategic solutions, and respond to rapidly changing events introduced throughout the session. Success in Ad Hoc depends on strong collaboration, persuasive speaking, and the ability to think on your feet, making it an exciting experience for both experienced delegates and those looking for a unique Model UN challenge.
